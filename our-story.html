Our Story

Everything started with an "It's me, hi" very Swiftie in 2023, the year my brother Diego and I were amazed at the cinema in Príncipe Pío, on October 13, 2023, in a packed theater watching The Eras Tour movie. We thought that was as close as we'd get to that spectacle, and that's when we both became Swifties.

May 2024 — "We hit the nail"

I had given up all hope. I didn't have a ticket and a magic Ticketmaster code arrived the same morning of the first concert, May 29, 2024. But I didn't see it until 4 PM, just as I was heading to my Generative AI class. I desperately wanted to buy it, but my brother, more pragmatic than a bridge in Cruel Summer, reminded me that getting to the Bernabéu at the last second was impossible. That "no" became the biggest Plot Twist of my life: the spark of KelceTS was born.

Generative AI Class — "You belong with AI"

That same afternoon I attended the course "Applications of Generative Artificial Intelligence" at Universidad Pontificia Comillas ICAI-ICADE. The practice consisted of creating an agent with ChatGPT for a European sneaker startup. In my head, the pieces fit together with a crash worthy of Style. What if we mixed fashion, sports, and the epic Swiftie narrative? Thus KelceTS was born, uniting the passion of Travis Kelce and the storytelling of Taylor Swift.

2025 — Prompts, Python, Power Ups... a Love Story with AI

Since that initial spark, KelceTS became my project laboratory: from analyzing complaints with AI to creating a multilingual customer experience system.

I invented a story about how KelceTS emerged as a fictional company on Oxford Street, London —one of my favorite streets where I've lived unforgettable moments— and used it in the Capstone Project of the Master's in Business AI at IIA and in the Google Kaggle Competition.

In 2025, KelceTS continued growing with me as I dove deep into the Copilot universe, creating conversational agents from scratch, orchestrating flows with LangGraph, and evaluating responses with RAGAS as part of my specialization in Generative AI. Between prompts, Python lines, and creative challenges, KelceTS was my testing ground, my portfolio, and my flag.

Additionally, with the boost from the Microsoft Power Up course, I dared to integrate a real agent within our website for the first time, designing an entire end-to-end experience with React, Node.js, and automatic deployment on Vercel.

Today, every challenge overcome and every app published sounds like a chorus of The Man, because although nobody bet on me... here I am, leading my own era.
